Cookie Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This policy explains how EICR in London uses cookies and similar storage or access technologies.

Essential technologies

Essential technologies support security, WordPress sessions, customer and staff sign-in, booking submission, payment protection and saved consent choices. These are used where necessary to provide a service you request or protect the website.

Analytics

If enabled and accepted, analytics helps us understand visits, popular pages and website performance. Analytics is not required to browse public pages or make a booking.

Google Ads campaign measurement

If enabled and accepted, Google Ads conversion measurement may record actions such as enquiries or completed bookings that follow a campaign visit. It does not create advertising space or display third-party ads on this website.

Connected sign-in and payments

Google, Apple, Facebook or Microsoft sign-in is contacted only when you choose the relevant option. Stripe resources are loaded on the booking page when card payment is configured and selected. Their own privacy and cookie terms also apply.

Managing choices

Use the Cookie settings link in the website footer to review or change optional choices. You can also manage cookies in your browser, although blocking essential technologies may prevent account, booking or payment features from working. For privacy questions, email info@eicrinlondon.co.uk.

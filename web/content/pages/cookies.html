Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains how EICR in London uses cookies and similar storage or access technologies. Optional cookies are only used after you agree in our cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time.

Essential cookies

These are needed for the website to work and cannot be switched off. They support security, enquiry and booking forms, payment protection and remembering your cookie choice.

  • eicr_consent – remembers your cookie choices for 6 months.

Analytics cookies (optional)

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics helps us understand visits, popular pages and website performance. It is not required to browse the website or make a booking.

  • _ga, _ga_* – Google Analytics, used to distinguish visits. Kept for up to 2 years.

Advertising cookies (optional)

If you accept advertising, Google Ads conversion measurement records actions such as enquiries or completed bookings that follow a visit from one of our Google adverts. This website does not show third-party adverts.

  • _gcl_au, _gcl_aw – Google Ads, used to measure advert results. Kept for up to 90 days.

We use Google Consent Mode, so Google tags are told about your choice and are not loaded until you accept analytics or advertising.

Payments

Card payments are taken on Stripe's secure payment page. Stripe's own privacy and cookie terms also apply.

Managing choices

Use the Cookie settings link (also in the website footer) to review or change optional choices. If you withdraw consent, we remove the Google cookies set on this website. You can also manage cookies in your browser, although blocking essential cookies may stop booking or payment features from working. For privacy questions, email info@eicrinlondon.co.uk.

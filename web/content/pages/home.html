Landlords: how often do I need an EICR and gas certificate?

In England, private landlords need an EICR at least every 5 years (or sooner if the last report says so) and a Gas Safety Certificate every 12 months. Give your tenants a copy of each. Book an EICR or book a gas certificate.

How quickly can I get an appointment?

Availability varies by service and location. Morning and afternoon options are offered where possible.

When will I receive my certificate?

Most reports are sent digitally after the inspection and the engineer has completed the required checks.

Can I book several services together?

Yes. Tell us which checks you need and we can help coordinate a suitable booking.
